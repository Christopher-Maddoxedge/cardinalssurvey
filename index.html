FAQ

Is this scientific?

No. It is a grassroots online survey. Respondents chose to take part (self-selected), so the results describe the people who responded and may not be generalized to all Cardinals fans.

Could people submit more than once?

No identity verification was required. 105 exact-copy responses were flagged, not removed. Dropping them moves no headline number by more than 1.1 points.

Who ran this?

Three Arizona Cardinals fans: Christopher Maddox (organizer and report author), with contributors Nathan Kropp and Sandi Van Lieu. It was created and run independently, by fans, for fans.

Is it affiliated with the team?

No. This survey is not affiliated with the Arizona Cardinals or the NFL.

What was masked in the data?

Profanity is masked with asterisks, slurs are shown as [slur removed], names of private individuals are masked, and seat locations and season-ticket or fan years are generalized in [brackets] (for example, [section] or [35+] years); “fan since” years are left as written. Everything else appears as written.

What happens next?

The report is being delivered to the Cardinals front office and the NFL.
